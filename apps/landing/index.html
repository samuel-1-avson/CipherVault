Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers without technical jargon:

Do I need to know about crypto or blockchain to use CipherVault?
Not at all. No wallet or tokens are required. CipherVault installs like standard developer tooling (PowerShell, Homebrew, Cargo) and uses familiar CLI commands like push and pull.
How is this different from putting .env in .gitignore?
.gitignore only stops files from being pushed to Git—it does not back them up or sync them. CipherVault securely backs up and syncs your secrets out-of-band.
Can anyone else see my passwords or API keys?
Never. Secrets are client-encrypted on your physical hardware before leaving your machine. Only your master key can decrypt them.
Is CipherVault free? Are there monthly subscription fees?
100% free and open-source under dual Apache-2.0 and MIT licenses. No monthly fees, no credit cards, zero vendor lock-in.
